How to use these calculators responsibly

Start with the balance, APR and payment amount from your latest statement. Use the fixed-payment tool to see how long your current payment might take. Next, use the target-date calculator to compare a shorter payoff plan. If you're considering a balance transfer, compare the transfer fee with interest saved during the introductory period and confirm the post-promotion APR separately.

Utilization is a ratio, not a guarantee about a credit score. The minimum-payment tool is a simplified scenario: real card issuers can use different minimum-payment formulas, add fees, and change rates. Always use the issuer's required minimum payment and statement disclosures for actual obligations.
